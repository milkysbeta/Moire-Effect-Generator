Electronics
Estimated 5 V budget≈ 1.25 A · 6.2 W Prototype supplyRegulated 5 V · 2 A

Left USB-C supplies power. Right USB-C accesses the ESP32-S3. Disconnect the ESP32’s main 5 V feed before plugging in a programming cable.

The ULN2003 boards share a low deck with the sockets facing up. The ESP32 sits upright on the right, with its USB-C face 0.6 mm inside the shoulder.

The upright USB-C openings are 9.2 × 16.8 mm. Route the motor plugs beneath the deck before seating the cards.

Assembly

The motor deck and disc guides print as one frame. Seat its two keys in the common electronics foot and secure it from underneath with two M2 × 5 countersunk screws.

Centre slot offset from S1000.00 mm

The frame sets the centre motor height. Slide it to take up belt slack. The lid has two smooth stops above each disc with 0.5 mm radial clearance.
