# Shared-base moiré sculpture parts checklist

Check each item against what you already have. Quantities below are for **two / three discs**. For the first fit test, make one motor station, one spindle with its two supports, one belt pair and a section of toothed rim.

## Drive hardware

Check that **72 mm × 3 mm** is a selectable combination and compare the total for three delivered to NZ. Many short loops are listed in 6 or 10 mm widths. The existing pulley face is 3.30 mm wide. - [ ] **4 / 6 matched 16T 2GT flanged pulleys and 4 / 6 M2 set screws**. Print one double-D motor pulley and one single-D Ø3 mm spindle pulley per station from [the editable CAD](../cad/moire-captive-drive.scad). The belt face is 3.3 mm wide. The flanges plus the screw boss make the pulley 6.5 mm deep. Confirm motor shaft dimensions, radial pilot fit and belt tracking with one pair. Off-the-shelf pulleys for 6 mm belts typically need a wider bay and different guard. [AliExpress 16T 2GT pulley search](https://www.aliexpress.com/wholesale?SearchText=2GT+16T+3mm+bore+3mm+wide+pulley) · [SDP/SI 16T pulley dimension drawing](https://shop.sdp-si.com/media/pdf/80502140.pdf)
- [ ] **2 / 3 smooth Ø3 mm spindles**. Start with straight, machinable round stock. Add a local flat at the rim cog and a D-flat along the rear pulley hub, leaving the journals round for the bearings and free idlers. Cut the shaft to suit the selected cartridge stack and front retainer. [100 mm rod from NZ Miniature Bearings](https://nzminiaturebearings.com/product/hss-linear-motion-shaft-rod-3mm-dia-100mm-length-sku-5503100560.html)
- [ ] **4 / 6 MR63ZZ bearings and 4 / 6 split bearing keepers**. The reference bearing is **3 mm ID × 6 mm OD × 2.5 mm wide**. Slide it into the stationary pocket against the 5.5 mm abutment, then seat the PETG keeper in its groove. Use the pick notch to remove it. The shoulder and keeper act on the bearing's outer race. Try the keeper and the 6.05, 6.15 and 6.25 mm bores in the fit coupon before printing the frame. Adjust the pocket to the actual measured bearing and printer fit. [EZO MR63ZZ dimensions](https://www.ezo-brg.co.jp/english/product/spec.php?eid=00179&unit=mm) · [AliExpress MR63ZZ search](https://www.aliexpress.com/wholesale?SearchText=MR63ZZ+3x6x2.5+bearings)

The shim and spacer have a nominal Ø3 mm ID and Ø3.6 mm face where they meet the inner races. Fit one stack before sizing the other shafts. Leave about 0.15 mm nominal endplay at the rear. ### Try one drive before buying parts for three

| Drive | What to try | Fit and motion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Printed pulleys and a bought belt** | Print one motor-end and one spindle-end 16T pulley in PLA+ using a dimensioned 2GT groove profile. Buy one **72-2GT-3** loop. | The belt runs on Ø12.1 mm pulley flanges, takes a small tension adjustment and preserves phase while the teeth stay engaged. Its compliance may soften high-frequency noise. Check for cyclic runout and lost motion with the actual printed parts. |
| **Two printed spur gears** | A pair of equal **25T, module 0.8** gears has 20 mm nominal centre distance and roughly Ø21.6 mm outside diameter per gear. Match pressure angles, set backlash and try one station. | One extra external mesh reverses motor direction while keeping the 10.333:1 reduction. The larger outlines need a revised cover and a clearance check. The current cover fits timing pulleys. |
| **Rubber band or small O-ring** | Try a smooth or grooved pulley on a hand-driven mock-up. | It can show the motion. Stretch and slipping can lose phase, especially during a reversal. Thick or tight bands also load the bearings. |

The **motor end** uses a double-D hub. Measure across the shaft round and flats and along the usable flat length. If it measures Ø5.0 mm across the round and 3.0 mm across the flats, try fit holes at 5.00/3.00, 5.10/3.10 and 5.20/3.20 mm before printing teeth. The flats carry torque and a recessed M2 set screw against a flat retains the hub axially. The **spindle pulley** uses a short matching flat and its own recessed M2 set screw. Seat it with the belt off. The **driven 12T pinion** now has a round slip bore so it can pass over the round bearing journals, with a proposed recessed M1.6 screw against a local shaft flat. Check that the small hub and its threads survive a fit coupon and loaded test. If the printed threads strip, make that hub in metal or revise the clamp. The preview's pulley grooves and screw details show the layout for that test. For a purchased pulley, check usable belt face, hub width and shaft fixing. [Gates 2GT profile and belt data](https://www.gates.com/content/dam/documents-library/catalogs/gates-unitta-asia-kr.pdf) · [KHK spur gear sizing](https://khkgears.net/pdf/2025/spur-gears.pdf)

For a **free idler only**, a round, straight, smooth nail or the smooth shoulder of a bolt can work as a test axle. Measure the shank in several places, deburr its end and trap the head and opposite end. Keep the thread outside the idler bore and bearing seats. A fully threaded screw makes a poor running surface. For the **driven spindle**, use a straight smooth blank with a keyed or positively clamped pulley and pinion. Measure any spare bearing **ID × OD × width** before choosing the axle and pocket. A free idler can run directly on a smooth journal with a little side clearance.

## Print or cut

- [ ] **One common electronics foot, one size-specific drive frame and two M2 × 5 countersunk screws**. Choose `common-base.stl` and the family, layer count and cartridge type in [the structure STL folder](../cad/skeleton/). The `cradle` and `common_base` parts in [the editable CAD](../cad/s100-skeleton.scad) have a `bed` setting for slicer work. The motor deck, bearing housings and gusseted guides print as one frame. The 1.10 mm guide tabs leave 0.20 mm clearance per adjacent face. Check the bed span for S250 and S300.
- [ ] **2 / 3 locked 12T drive cogs with M1.6 screws and 2 / 4 free 12T idlers**. Choose the printed or Perspex cartridge version from [the prototype STL folder](../cad/drive-parts/). Each cog has two tapered lips. The idlers have Ø3.30 mm bores. Use **2 / 4 0.20 mm hub shims** between adjacent cogs and **4 / 6 end sleeves** sized to the measured stack. In the three-disc version the front C sleeve is a long spacer tube. Check hand rotation with each disc seated.
- [ ] **2 / 3 S100 cartridges**. Print each whole 2.4 mm cartridge, or combine a printed 124T perimeter carrier with a nominal 3 mm cast-acrylic pattern insert, front shoulder and separate back keeper. 3.6 mm is the modeled hybrid cartridge thickness. For the fit coupon, the insert OD is Ø94.4 mm, seat ID Ø94.8 mm, and keeper opening Ø93.2 mm. Check sheet thickness and kerf on the actual parts. Use 3.9 mm lane spacing for printed or 5.1 mm for hybrid cartridges. The pattern insert preview does not supply a cutter-ready DXF. [NZ 3 mm cast acrylic](https://laserlinx.co.nz/item/standard/) · [NZ acrylic cutting service](https://www.makeshop.co.nz/lasercutting)
- [ ] **Diameter-specific surround, neck panel and lift-off lid**, plus the **shared fascia, rear cover and clips**. The visible front skin keeps the shaft ends recessed. The lid uses two gravity-seated tongues and pockets. Try PETG for reusable spring fingers and test the clearance before printing the full shell.
- [ ] **2 optional TPU feet or rubber pads**. TPU suits the shelf feet, cable grommets and light anti-rattle pads. On the motors, a thin damping pad can sit against a hard locating stop. Keep soft material out of the load path that controls belt tension and gear mesh.
- [ ] **Optional wall kit**. Metal support shelf, interlocking metal cleat, lower anti-lift keeper, 2 M4 through-fasteners with backing washers on 50 mm centres, and wall fasteners selected for the finished assembly and substrate. The shared base STL has the floor holes. The cleat and metal bracket require a physical load check before installation.
- [ ] **Optional edge-lit cassette**. Size-specific 2 mm opal acrylic disc, 3 mm patterned light guide, white reflector, perimeter LED modules and separate low-voltage power branch. S250 uses a nominal Ø238.1 mm diffuser. The viewer shows the fit of the layers. Check optical uniformity and temperature on a sample.

## Electronics and wiring

- [ ] **2 / 3 stock 5 V 28BYJ-48 five-wire motors and ULN2003 boards**. The enclosure uses the Kiatronics motor drawing as a reference and a provisional 35 × 32 mm PCB from the sibling project. Compare the motor label, ear centres, shaft flats and projection. On the driver, check board width and height, hole centres, the direction of the keyed socket and the overall height with the plugs installed. If your kit arrived with a larger board, resize or stagger the tray before making the enclosure. [Your AliExpress motor listing](https://www.aliexpress.com/item/1005009331799965.html) · [Motor datasheet](https://altronics.cl/uploads/28BYJ-48.pdf) · [ULN2003A datasheet](https://www.ti.com/product/ULN2003A)
- [ ] **Two M2 × 4 screws per ULN2003 board**. Each card sits sockets-up on four low standoffs with 30.3 × 27.3 mm reference hole centres. Install two diagonal screws and check the depth against the underside of the foot. For service, unplug the headers and draw the board out to the rear after removing the screws.
- [ ] **1 ESP32-S3 SuperMini**, right-hand USB-C data cable, upright edge rails, removable edge retainers and a disconnect in the main 5 V feed. Disconnect the header plugs with the side shell off, then release the retainers and slide the board out to the right. The model uses the 23.5 × 18 mm SuperMini envelope and a reference 2.54 mm pin pitch. Both USB-C cutouts are 8.4 mm deep by 16 mm high for an upright 6.5 × 14 mm example plug. Measure the actual plug, board and socket offset before cutting the shells. [Sibling firmware wiring](../../Moire-Effect-Generator-Smooth/firmware/README.md)
- [ ] **1 regulated 5 V supply rated at least 2 A**, with a matched USB-C power input module and cable. Use a sink and source rated to deliver the current at 5 V. A breakout offering selectable higher voltages must be configured and measured at 5 V before wiring the motors or ESP32. A Jaycar HUSB238 board is an example for the bench, with its exact PCB mount to be set after measurement. The model shows a generic compact board envelope. [Jaycar NZ HUSB238 breakout](https://www.jaycar.co.nz/usb-c-power-delivery-dummy-breakout-i2c-or-fixed-husb238/p/PP2081) · [Adafruit HUSB238 configuration guide](https://learn.adafruit.com/adafruit-husb238-usb-type-c-power-delivery-breakout)
- [ ] **Power splitter, hookup wire, plug/header leads, heat-shrink, and harness clips/grommets**. The open gap under the compact motor deck is 8.6 mm high. Feed the plugs through before fitting the motor-driver cards. Leave an accessible loop for the removable ESP32 and tie the looms clear of the belts. Check the actual keyed motor plugs and cable bends against the space. [JST XH connector datasheet](https://www.jst-mfg.com/product/pdf/eng/eXH.pdf)
- [ ] **Optional pause button or lid interlock and separate lighting allowance**. The existing S3 configuration puts the panel button on GPIO 13 to ground. Treat lid sensing as a separate firmware feature and stop the drive before disc swaps. Add LED current to the budget when the lighting is chosen.

Each five-wire motor plugs into its own ULN2003. From the ESP32-S3, connect IN1–IN4 on A to GPIO 1–4, B to GPIO 5–8 and C to GPIO 9–12. The left USB-C inlet feeds all ULN2003 boards and the board's verified 5 V input through the service disconnect. Join grounds. Before plugging a computer into the ESP32's right-side USB-C port, unplug the left supply and open the ESP32 5 V feed so the data cable cannot back-feed the driver rail. Verify the pinout and power path on your exact S3 board.

**Wiring & parts** opens the diagram and shows the reference connector locations. **Trace a connection** isolates an individual GPIO-to-IN lead, a whole motor plug, the 5 V supply or common ground. **Show wire labels** controls the endpoint labels. Orange, yellow, green and blue identify IN1–IN4. Red shows 5 V and grey shows ground. Compare the S3 and ULN2003 silkscreens before wiring.

The datasheet gives 50 Ω ±7% per winding. At 5 V and two energized windings per motor, use 5/46.5 × 2 = 0.215 A per motor as a planning value, before the ULN2003 voltage drop. Adding 0.50 A controller allowance and 0.10 A small-load margin gives **1.03 A for two motors** or **1.25 A for three**, roughly **5.2 or 6.2 W** at 5 V. The 0.50 A is a budget, not a measurement. Check motor voltage and input current with all channels holding and starting. [Motor datasheet](https://altronics.cl/uploads/28BYJ-48.pdf) · [Espressif ESP32-S3 hardware guide](https://docs.espressif.com/projects/esp-hardware-design-guidelines/en/latest/esp32s3/schematic-checklist.html)

## Speed and assembly checks

Use **16T motor / 16T spindle** at 20 mm centres. The open belt stage is 1:1 and the 12T pinion against the 124T rim gives **10.333:1** overall. At 240 seconds per front-disc turn, speeds in A/B/C order are **+0.25 / −0.50 / +0.75 disc rpm** and **−2.58 / +5.17 / −7.75 motor output rpm** by the viewer's sign convention. Gentle drift at ±25% peaks at 9.69 rpm, below the sibling firmware's 12 rpm setting. A 20T spindle pulley raises reduction to 12.917:1. At that same drift and turn time the fastest output just exceeds 12 rpm, and a 72 mm belt would need about 18 mm centres. Changing pulley sizes means a different slot, belt length, or both. Try 3, 6 and 9 motor rpm under load before changing the reduction. The unloaded motor noise spec gives no installed noise target.

1. Bolt the size-specific cradle to the shared base. Before fitting the cover, check that both journal bores share the same axis.
2. Fit the bearings and housing keepers, then feed each spindle through its shims and cogs. Fit its front retaining clip. Turn the shaft to access the pinion screw. Lock the pinion and rear pulley to their flats, check endplay, then fit and tension the belt. Turn each free cog by hand.
3. Seat the two USB boards and lower the motor-driver boards into their pockets. Fit the M2 keepers, then route and anchor the loom. Test the supply and motion before fitting the slip-on belt covers and rear shell.
4. Seat the front fascia on its guides and press the hidden catches home. Check the 1.4 mm front skin against the recessed shaft ends and run a dry-fit clip test. Stand it upright, drop the cartridges into the guide combs and line up their phase marks.
5. Drop the top lid into the two pockets. Check that its underside clears the teeth at rest and limits upward travel. Check that the discs can be lifted clear with the lid removed and the drive stopped.
